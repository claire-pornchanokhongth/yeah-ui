Member portal — login, registration, member hub

Grey areas = photo placeholders · TH/EN toggle in the header (TH default) · the portal reuses the landing page's palette, type and chrome

Desktop and mobile are two separate files, as the PRD requires. Mobile uses a five-step wizard with bottom sheets for every long option list and a section menu under the profile block; desktop uses one dense form and a sticky side rail. Use the tabs above to jump between the three screens; the Google button on login plays a sign-in transition and lands on registration, and finishing registration lands on the member hub. The two platforms deliberately tell different stories at that moment — use the replay buttons to watch either one.

Data contract. Every input carries the field name the backend receives, and every rendered record carries its id — data-program-id, data-application-id, data-participation-id, data-field — so the same data can be read out of the markup without any of it being visible on screen. Full field list, enums and endpoints: YEAH Portal Data Contract.md.

Sign-in transition. Clicking through on the login screen plays a full-screen transition before the auth step. Mobile launches a pink rocket that climbs off the top of the screen and fills the frame with its exhaust (2.2s flight, white at 2.65s). Desktop flies the yellow key element in from off-screen, threads it through the keyhole in the A, turns it 90° so the round bow goes edge-on and leaves the straight bar of the unlocked wordmark, then rips a horizontal flare across the frame that blooms into the loading screen (white at 1.3s). Both then hold for 850ms, standing in for the real Google round-trip — that hold is the only number to change when live auth goes in. Both respect prefers-reduced-motion, which skips to a still mark and a short wash.

Deep links. Both files accept ?screen=login|register|hub and &launch=1, which auto-plays the sign-in transition once on load (this is what the replay buttons use); mobile also takes &tab=status|programs|history|profile and &step=1–5.
